# Carbon Credit Quality Red Flags

Indicators that a carbon project may be low quality:

## Baseline Issues
- Unrealistically high baseline emissions
- Lack of historical data
- Ignoring regulatory trends

## Additionality Concerns
- Activity already common practice
- Profitable without carbon revenue
- Required by law

## MRV Weakness
- Sparse monitoring data
- Infrequent verification
- High uncertainty

## Permanence Risks
- High fire or land-use pressure
- Weak land tenure
- No buffer reserve

## Leakage Risks
- Displacement likely but unaccounted
- Narrow project boundary

Multiple red flags indicate elevated credit risk.
