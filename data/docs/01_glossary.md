# Carbon Credit Glossary

## Carbon Credit
A tradable certificate representing the reduction or removal of one metric ton of CO₂ equivalent (tCO₂e) from the atmosphere.

## Additionality
The principle that emission reductions would not have occurred without the carbon project and its financing.

## Baseline
The estimated emissions that would occur in the absence of the project.

## MRV (Monitoring, Reporting, Verification)
The process used to measure, document, and independently verify emission reductions.

## Leakage
Unintended increase in emissions outside the project boundary caused by project activities.

## Permanence
The durability of carbon sequestration over time and the risk of reversal (e.g., forest fires).

## Vintage
The year in which emission reductions occurred.

## Retirement
Permanent removal of a carbon credit from the market after use to offset emissions.

## Co-benefits
Additional environmental or social benefits beyond carbon reduction (e.g., biodiversity, community income).

## Double Counting
The same emission reduction claimed by more than one entity.
