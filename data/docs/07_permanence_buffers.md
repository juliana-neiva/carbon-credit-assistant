# Permanence and Buffer Mechanisms

Permanence refers to the long-term stability of carbon storage.

Nature-based projects face reversal risks:

- Wildfire
- Disease
- Illegal logging
- Land-use change

## Buffer Pools

Many standards require withholding a percentage of credits in a shared buffer reserve.

If reversal occurs:
buffer credits are cancelled to compensate.

## Risk Assessment Factors

- Fire risk
- Governance quality
- Monitoring strength
- Land tenure security

Low permanence increases credit risk.
