# Carbon Credit Project Scorecard Template

A structured evaluation framework for carbon project quality.

## Dimensions

### Additionality
High: clearly dependent on carbon finance  
Medium: partially dependent  
Low: likely non-additional  

### Baseline Credibility
High: conservative, data-backed  
Medium: reasonable assumptions  
Low: inflated or uncertain  

### MRV Robustness
High: frequent, precise monitoring  
Medium: standard monitoring  
Low: weak or uncertain  

### Permanence Risk
Low: durable storage, safeguards  
Medium: some reversal risk  
High: significant reversal risk  

### Leakage Risk
Low: negligible displacement  
Medium: possible displacement  
High: likely displacement  

### Co-benefits
High: strong social/ecological gains  
Medium: moderate benefits  
Low: minimal  

## Overall Rating
High / Medium / Low quality carbon credit

## Analyst Notes
Free-text justification referencing evidence.
