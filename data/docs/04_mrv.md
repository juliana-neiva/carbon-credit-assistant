# MRV: Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification

MRV ensures that carbon reductions are measurable and credible.

## Monitoring
Continuous or periodic measurement of relevant parameters such as biomass growth, energy generation, or fuel use.

## Reporting
Compilation of monitoring data into standardized reports following the methodology.

## Verification
Independent audit confirming that reported emission reductions are accurate and compliant.

## Key MRV Principles

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Transparency
- Conservativeness

Weak MRV increases uncertainty and credit risk.
