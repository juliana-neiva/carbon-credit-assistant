# Leakage in Carbon Projects

Leakage occurs when emission reductions inside the project cause emissions elsewhere.

## Examples

- Protecting one forest shifts deforestation to another area
- Efficient cookstoves increase charcoal production elsewhere
- Agricultural land protection expands farming into new land

## Leakage Types

- Activity-shifting leakage
- Market leakage

## Mitigation

- Expanding project boundary
- Monitoring surrounding regions
- Applying leakage deduction factors

Unaccounted leakage reduces real climate benefit.
