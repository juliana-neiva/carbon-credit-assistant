# Carbon Credit Project Lifecycle

Carbon credit projects typically follow a standardized lifecycle:

## 1. Project Design
The developer defines the project activity, boundary, baseline scenario, and methodology.

## 2. Validation
An independent auditor reviews the Project Design Document (PDD) to confirm compliance with the standard.

## 3. Implementation
The project is executed (e.g., forest protection, renewable installation).

## 4. Monitoring
Data on emission reductions are collected according to the MRV plan.

## 5. Verification
An accredited third party verifies monitored emission reductions.

## 6. Issuance
Verified reductions are converted into carbon credits and issued in a registry.

## 7. Trading
Credits may be sold in voluntary or compliance markets.

## 8. Retirement
Credits are retired when used to offset emissions.

Each stage ensures environmental integrity and market transparency.
