# Double Counting in Carbon Markets

Double counting happens when the same emission reduction is claimed more than once.

## Types

### Double Issuance
Two credits issued for the same reduction.

### Double Use
Same credit used by multiple buyers.

### Double Claiming
Both host country and buyer country claim the reduction.

## Prevention Mechanisms

- Unique serial numbers
- Public registries
- Corresponding adjustments (Article 6)

Double counting undermines market integrity.
